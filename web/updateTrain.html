Train Update Process

Step 1: Enter the train number of the train that needs to be updated.
Step 2: Enter the new train information.
Step 3: The system searches for the train in the MySQL database.
Step 4: The selected train record is updated with the new information.
Admin Function: Train information management and database update.

Important

Enter the correct train number before updating the train.

Updating the train details may also reset the available seat count according to the current admin implementation.
