Ticket Cancellation

Cancel Ticket

Enter your PNR number to cancel the corresponding railway ticket.




Cancellation Process

Step 1: The system searches for the booking using the PNR number.
Step 2: The booking status is changed to CANCELLED.
Step 3: If a waiting-list passenger exists, the first passenger in the queue can be promoted to CONFIRMED.

Important

Please enter the correct PNR number.

A cancelled ticket cannot be used for travel.
