Join Waiting List

If seats are not available, you can add your booking request to the waiting list.






















Waiting List Process

Step 1: Passenger details are entered into the waiting-list form.
Step 2: The passenger is added to the waiting queue.
Step 3: A queue position is assigned to the passenger.
Step 4: When a confirmed ticket is cancelled, the first waiting passenger can be promoted.

Important

Waiting-list passengers are processed according to their queue position.

The passenger who joins first gets the opportunity first when a seat becomes available.
